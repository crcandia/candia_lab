<p style="text-align: center;">CRiSS-LAB is an interdisciplinary computational social science laboratory at Universidad del Desarrollo in Santiago, Chile, directed by Cristian Candia, Ph.D. We study human behavior and social systems using large-scale data, experiments, network science, statistical modeling, causal inference, and artificial intelligence.</p>

<p style="text-align: center;">Our research spans collective memory and attention, cooperation and learning, social networks, preferences and decision-making, computational models of human heterogeneity, and the interaction between people and AI. We also turn research into applied platforms and evidence for education, science, organizations, public-interest technology, and other real-world settings.</p>
