Sputnik News reported on an analysis by Cristian Candia-Castro and colleagues that used the number of Wikipedia language editions covering a person as a proxy for international fame. The analysis put the apparent wave of celebrity deaths in 2016 in the context of a growing pool of people who meet that definition.
