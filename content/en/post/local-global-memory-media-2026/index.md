On October 26, 2026, Constructor University published a feature and interview about the PNAS Nexus study by Mariana Macedo, Melanie Oyarzun, Cristian Candia and César A. Hidalgo. The coverage examines differences between local and global recognition and how attention changes around major life events. The same institutional feature appeared on Phys.org and IDW, including a German version.
