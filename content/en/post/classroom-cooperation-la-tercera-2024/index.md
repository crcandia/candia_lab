On September 17, 2024, *Qué Pasa* in *La Tercera* covered two Universidad del Desarrollo studies that used a game to measure cooperation in primary school classrooms. One study linked reciprocal cooperation to academic performance. The other found that students who received less cooperation were more likely to be identified as both victims and perpetrators of bullying. The article quotes Cristian Candia on what the findings suggest about encouraging cooperation in the classroom.

**Original studies:** [Classroom reciprocity and academic performance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2022.e11916) and [cooperation and bullying](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-024-03493-5).
