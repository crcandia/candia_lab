The September 22, 2026 issue of *Research + Insights*, Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management newsletter, highlighted *Saturation of moral language predicts lower content engagement on social media*. The newsletter points readers to the study, led by Cristian Candia and co-authored with Mohammad Atari, Nour Kteily and Brian Uzzi, in its roundup of recently published research.

The researchers analyzed about 1.6 million observations from Twitter, Reddit and 8chan. They distinguished a post’s overall moral relevance from the concentration of moral language in it: these two aspects showed different relationships with engagement.

[Read the study in Nature Human Behaviour](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02560-y).
