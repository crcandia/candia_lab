On July 8, 2025, Vicente Rotman interviewed Cristian Candia on the Spanish-language podcast *No tan Humanos*. Candia explains how Capybara uses a game to observe how children cooperate and give school staff a better picture of classroom relationships. The discussion also covers DYNAMAP’s approach to mapping political preferences and asks how people can establish trust online as deepfakes become more convincing.
