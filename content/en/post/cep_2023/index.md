[**Original source: Estudios Públicos**](https://estudiospublicos.cl/index.php/cep/article/view/2212)

Cristian Candia and María Paz Raveau discuss the promise and limits of deliberative democracy through Chile’s experience from 2015 to 2022. They argue that designing a participatory process is only part of the work: political incentives, institutions and the wider social context also shape what it can achieve. The accompanying commentary considers how collective intelligence and digital tools might support deliberation.
