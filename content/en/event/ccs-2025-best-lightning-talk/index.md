Cristian Candia presented **“Neural Network Nominate (NNN): Mapping Mass Political Ideology via Revealed Preferences”** in the lightning-talk session of the **Conference on Complex Systems 2025** in Siena, Italy.

The method uses millions of pairwise policy choices to infer the structure of political preferences with an interpretable neural-network architecture, providing an alternative to direct ideological self-placement and traditional legislative scaling approaches.

The presentation received the **Best Lightning Talk Award at CCS 2025**.

The official CCS programme lists Cristian Candia in the Tuesday, September 2 lightning-talk session, scheduled from 10:30 to 11:00. Each lightning talk lasts three minutes.
