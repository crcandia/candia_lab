Cristian Candia participated as **Invited Faculty** in the 2025 Complexity Global School for Emerging Political Economies, organized by the **Santa Fe Institute** and **Universidad de los Andes** in Bogotá.

The school brought together participants from academia, government, the private sector, and civil society to work with complexity-based approaches to economic, political, and social systems.

During the program, Cristian presented CRiSS-LAB research and applied projects spanning political preferences, collective behavior, education, and data-driven public-interest platforms. He also participated in small-group discussions and faculty interactions focused on connecting complexity science with real institutional problems.
