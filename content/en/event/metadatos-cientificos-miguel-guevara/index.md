## Using data to connect science and industry

How can we identify research teams and connect their expertise with industry needs?

**Miguel Guevara**, from **Universidad de Playa Ancha**, will present **ApiRipa and EqMetrics**, two applications developed under FONDEF Technological Research Project 2024 **IT24I0145**. These tools aim to support the formation and characterization of research teams and facilitate their connections with companies and other organizations interested in open innovation, R&D, and collaboration with academia.

The talk will also explore research opportunities offered by data collected from **Google Scholar, OpenAlex, ANID's Researcher Portal, and INAPI**.

**Original talk title:** Plataforma de metadatos científicos para la colaboración en I+D+i: Resultados del Proyecto FONDEF Investigación Tecnológica 2024 IT24I0145.
