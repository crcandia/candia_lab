## Working with agents: when “done” needs evidence

On **July 20, 2026**, Cristian Candia participated in **Codex for Data Science** at Universidad del Desarrollo. The workshop took place during OpenAI Build Week and marked the Data Science Institute’s tenth anniversary.

His closing presentation addressed an architecture for agent-based research workflows, emphasizing reusable procedures, review of outputs, and evidence that allows work to be continued and defended. Its original title is *Trabajo con agentes: cuando «listo» necesita pruebas*.

The workshop also included a presentation by **Germán Gómez-Vargas** on data science workflows and a practical session led by **Alonso Astroza**.

**Full workshop schedule:** 09:00–15:00, Santiago time (UTC−4).  
**Venue:** AFP Cuprum Auditorium (JPOSTB), level −1 of the Postgraduate Building, Rector Ernesto Silva Bafalluy campus, Av. Plaza 680, Las Condes. In-person attendance and an online livestream.
